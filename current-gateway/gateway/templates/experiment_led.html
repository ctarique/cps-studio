Create Your Own LED Program


    

      Create a small program that controls the sequence and timing of the LED.
    



    


      


      
Supported Operations


      


        

          
led(1)

          
Turn the LED on

        


        

          
led(0)

          
Turn the LED off

        


        

          
wait_ms(N)

          
Wait between 10 and 1000 milliseconds

        


        

          
status()

          
Request device status

  • Maximum 32 operations
    • 
        
  • Maximum 5 seconds of total wait time

  • 
          Programs using the LED must end with
          led(0)
